1. About GeoRoots

GeoRoots ("the App", "we", "us", "our") is a field-mapping application developed by students of Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Coimbatore, under faculty guidance. It grew out of the Amrita Live-in-Labs® programme. It lets teams record real infrastructure on the ground (water taps, road damage, open drainage, streetlights, civic hazards, public assets, transformers, EV points, hospitals, ration shops, shelters and risk zones) with GPS coordinates, notes and photos, and see them on a shared map.

This policy explains every kind of personal information the App handles, why, who can see it, how long we keep it, which outside services it touches, and what choices you have. It applies to the GeoRoots Android app and any web version. It is written to follow the Google Play User Data policy and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

3. Information we collect

3.1 When you register

  • Account details: user ID, phone number (with country code), and a password. Your password is never stored as typed. It is converted into a salted PBKDF2-SHA256 hash on your device before it is sent.
  • Full name exactly as printed on your Aadhaar card.
  • Image of the QR side of your Aadhaar card (compressed JPEG). We do not ask for, and do not store, your Aadhaar number. An older field for the last four digits is no longer collected and is left empty.
  • A live photo of yourself taken with your camera at registration. Photos from your gallery are not accepted.
  • Role selection: whether you are registering as a student or faculty member, if you tick the Amrita option.
  • Amrita users only: your institutional email address (for example an Outlook address), your affiliation, a signature you draw on screen (stored as an image), the version of the consent form you signed, and the date and time you signed it.
  • Registration status and the time of registration and of approval.

3.2 When you sign in and use the App

  • Remember-this-device record: a random token stored on your device, and a one-way SHA-256 hash of it stored on our server with its creation time, last-seen time and an expiry 90 days after creation. This keeps you signed in so you are not asked for your password each time.
  • Your last used user ID, saved on your device to pre-fill the sign-in box.
  • Preferences: theme (dark or light), outdoor high-contrast setting, and which map layers and modes you switch on.

3.3 Survey points you log

  • Category (such as water tap, road damage, drainage, streetlight, hazard, public asset, transformer, EV point, hospital, ration shop, shelter, risk zone) and any custom category label
  • Latitude, longitude, ellipsoidal height and the GNSS quality stamp: fix type, HDOP, number of satellites, estimated accuracy, correction station name and age
  • Date and time
  • Your free-text notes
  • Photos you attach, optionally stamped with the category logo and name
  • Your phone number and your user ID, saved with the point. The phone number is kept for records and for sending you the confirmation SMS. The user ID records ownership and controls who can delete the point.

3.4 Location

Described fully in section 9. In brief: your position while the App is open, and, only if you turn it on, a shared live location and trail for administrators.

3.5 Messages

Messages sent in the in-app chat between administrators, with the sender, the conversation, the time and whether the message has been read.

3.6 Administrator activity

For administrators only, we keep an audit log of administrative actions: which administrator, what action, which user it concerned and when. It exists to keep the system accountable.

3.7 Exports

Export requests from users (who asked, when, and for which approved time window) and the administrator's approval or rejection.

3.8 Data from your actions elsewhere in the App

  • Categories: when an administrator renames, recolours or removes a category, the change is stored with who made it and when.
  • Admin contact preferences: a faculty member's choice of whether GeoRoots sends SMS alerts to their phone.
  • GNSS inputs: if you use NMEA / GNSS logger mode, the NMEA sentences you paste are processed on your device to compute a position.
We do not collect: your SMS messages, call history, contacts, microphone recordings, files from your gallery, your Aadhaar number, financial information, or advertising identifiers. We do not collect your location while the App is closed.

6. How we use information

  • To create, verify and manage accounts, and to prevent fake or duplicate registrations
  • To keep you signed in on your trusted device
  • To provide mapping, positioning, routing, weather and hazard, logging and export features
  • To store and share survey points within the right group
  • To let administrators coordinate field teams, only if you share your location
  • To send account, approval and point-confirmation notifications
  • To keep administrators accountable through the audit log
  • To keep the service secure and fix faults
  • For community, disaster-preparedness and academic research using survey points, never identity documents
  • To comply with law and respond to valid legal requests

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, automated decisions or sale. If we want to use it for a new purpose, we will ask for your consent first.

8. Identity verification (Aadhaar QR and live photo)

GeoRoots is used for real field surveys, so administrators approve every new account before it can be used. For this we collect your name as on your Aadhaar card, an image of the QR side of the card, and a live camera photo.

  • The information is used only to confirm that you are who you say you are.
  • It is shown only to authorised administrators on the approval screen. It is never shown to other users.
  • It is never used for marketing, research, or sharing with other organisations, except where the law requires.
  • We do not collect your Aadhaar number, and we do not use Aadhaar for authentication or e-KYC with UIDAI.
  • If you are rejected, we delete the documents within 30 days.
  • If you are approved, you can ask us to delete the documents at any time (section 14).

If you are not comfortable providing these documents, please do not register.

9. Location in detail

  1. While the App is open your device location is read to centre the map, show "My position", calculate routes, find nearby shelters and campuses, fetch local weather, and stamp coordinates on points you save. A point's coordinates are frozen at the moment you open the log screen.
  2. Location Sharing (optional). If you switch on Location Sharing in "My Location", your latest position (latitude, longitude, accuracy, time) is saved as your single current-location record, and a new entry is added to your location history whenever you move a meaningful distance or enough time passes. Administrators see this on the User Locations panel. You can turn it off at any time. When you turn it off, we stop collecting and update your record to show sharing is disabled.
  3. Location history can be viewed by administrators by range (today, 7 days, 30 days or custom) and can be shown as a route on the map.
  4. No background tracking. The App does not collect your location when it is closed.

10. SMS and email

GeoRoots sends the following automated messages:

  • Registration received and registration approved messages, to the number you registered with
  • A confirmation when you save a point, containing the category, the coordinates and a point ID, sent to the phone number saved with your point
  • New-registration and approval alerts to administrators and to a faculty member, who can switch these on or off

How they are sent. The App does not send text messages from your phone. When a message is triggered, a row containing the destination phone number and the message text is placed in a queue on our server. A separate GeoRoots relay device, kept by the project team, reads the queue and sends the message using its own SIM. The queue entry is deleted after the message is picked up. Messages therefore pass through our server and the relay device, and then your mobile operator.

Normal operator rules apply. If you do not want these messages, contact us (section 23).

11. Who can see your information

  • Administrators can see registration details, identity documents, all survey points (with reporter phone and user ID), messages between administrators, shared locations and the audit log.
  • Other users see survey points of their own group only. They see the point details, notes and photos. They do not see your identity documents, password or shared location.
  • Exports are controlled. Non-administrators cannot download data on their own. An administrator approves each request for a date and time window, and users can export only their own group's points.
  • Authorities and researchers. Aggregated or de-identified infrastructure data may be shared with local authorities, partner institutions or researchers for civic and research purposes. Identity documents and passwords are never included.
  • Service providers in section 12, only as needed to run the App.
  • Legal reasons. We may disclose information if required by law or court order, or to protect someone's safety.

We do not sell personal data and do not share it for a purpose facilitating sale.

15. Security

  • All connections to our server use encrypted HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored only as salted PBKDF2-SHA256 hashes. The remember-this-device token is stored on our server only as a SHA-256 hash.
  • Registration is approval-based, and identity documents are displayed only on the administrator approval screen.
  • The database is protected by row-level security rules, and the two datasets (Amrita and public) are separated.
  • Exports are locked by default and require administrator approval for a time window.
  • Administrator actions are recorded in an audit log.
  • Photos on your device are not saved to your gallery unless you export them.

No online system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your password private and tell us straight away if you think your account was misused. If a personal-data breach affects you, we will notify you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

16. Where data is stored

Data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, on servers that may be outside India. Map, weather and routing providers in section 12 are also located in other countries. By using the App you understand that your information may be processed outside India. We choose providers with recognised security practices.

17. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable law, you may:

  • ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and who it has been shared with
  • ask us to correct or complete inaccurate data
  • ask us to delete your data, or withdraw your consent at any time
  • turn off location sharing in the App, and revoke device permissions in Settings
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights if you are unable to
  • make a grievance to us. If it is not resolved, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Send requests to the contact in section 23. We aim to reply within 30 days. We may need to confirm your identity first.

18. Consent

We process your information based on your consent. You give it when you create an account and tick the agreement, and again when you turn on optional features such as Location Sharing or grant a device permission. Amrita students and faculty also sign a separate consent form in the App, which we record with a version number. You can withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing it does not affect earlier processing, but we may then be unable to keep your account.

19. Children

GeoRoots is for adults aged 18 and over, and for students and faculty of higher-education institutions. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18. If you believe a child has registered, tell us and we will delete the account.

20. Weather and hazard information

Weather, flood, wave, air-quality and earthquake information in the App comes from third parties and is shown for general awareness. It is not an official warning. In an emergency, follow instructions from IMD, NDMA, your state disaster management authority and local officials.
