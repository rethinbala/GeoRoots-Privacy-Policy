1. About GeoRoots

GeoRoots ("the App", "we", "us", "our") is a field-mapping application developed by students of Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Coimbatore, under faculty guidance, and grew out of the Amrita Live-in-Labs® programme. It lets teams record real infrastructure on the ground (water taps, road damage, drainage, streetlights, shelters, hazards and similar points) with GPS coordinates, notes and photos, and see them on a shared map.

This policy explains what personal information the App handles, why, who can see it, how long we keep it, and what choices you have. It applies to the GeoRoots Android app and any web version. It is written to meet the Google Play User Data policy and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you provide when you register

  • User ID and password
  • Full name, as printed on your Aadhaar card
  • Phone number (with country code)
  • An image of the QR-code side of your Aadhaar card (you never need to type your Aadhaar number)
  • A live photo of yourself taken with the camera at registration. Photos from your gallery are not accepted.
  • If you are an Amrita student or faculty member: your institutional email address, whether you are a student or faculty, and a signed consent form

2.2 Survey data you create

  • Infrastructure points: category, GPS coordinates, altitude, GNSS quality details (fix type, HDOP, satellite count, estimated accuracy), date and time, notes and attached photos
  • Your user ID and user group (Amrita or general public) attached to each point
  • Messages you exchange with administrators in the in-app chat

2.3 Location data

  • During use: your device location is used to centre the map, calculate routes, find nearby points and stamp coordinates on points you log.
  • Optional location sharing: if you switch on "Location Sharing", your live location and location history are sent to administrators so field work can be coordinated. It is off by default, requires your explicit action, and can be turned off at any time.
  • The App does not track your location when it is closed.

2.4 Technical information

  • Basic connection and app information needed to keep the App working and synced
  • Preferences saved on your device, such as theme and sign-in session (see section 10)
We do not collect: your SMS messages, call history, contacts, microphone recordings, files from your gallery, or financial information. We do not use advertising identifiers.

4. How we use your information

  • To create, verify and manage accounts, and to prevent fake or duplicate registrations
  • To provide the mapping, logging, routing and export features
  • To let administrators coordinate field teams, if you choose to share your location
  • To send account, approval and security notifications
  • To improve the accuracy and reliability of the App
  • For community, disaster-preparedness and academic research on civic infrastructure, using survey data and not identity documents
  • To comply with legal obligations and respond to valid legal requests

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or sale, and we use it only for the purposes above. If we want to use it for a new purpose, we will ask for your consent first.

6. Identity verification (Aadhaar QR and live photo)

GeoRoots is used for real field surveys, so administrators approve every new account before it can be used. For this we ask for the following:

  • An image of the QR side of your Aadhaar card and your name as printed on it
  • A live camera photo of yourself

This information is used only to confirm that you are who you say you are. It is viewable only by authorised GeoRoots administrators, is never shown to other users, is never used for marketing or research, and is not shared with third parties except where required by law. We do not ask you to type your Aadhaar number, and we do not use Aadhaar for authentication or e-KYC with UIDAI.

Because Aadhaar information is sensitive, you can ask us to delete the image and photo once your account is approved, or at any time, by writing to the contact in section 18. If you do not want to provide these documents, please do not register.

7. Who can see your information

  • Administrators (the GeoRoots team and faculty) can see registration details, identity documents, all survey points, messages and any location you have chosen to share.
  • Other users see survey points according to their group: Amrita students and faculty see Amrita points, and general-public users see public points. They do not see your identity documents, phone number or location.
  • Exports are controlled. Users cannot download data on their own. An administrator must approve each request for a time window, and users can export only their own group's points.
  • Authorities and researchers: aggregated or de-identified infrastructure data may be shared with local authorities, partner institutions or researchers for civic and research purposes. Identity documents are never included.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information where required by law, court order, or to protect safety.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for a purpose facilitating sale.

8. Third-party services

The App uses these providers to work. Each handles data under its own privacy policy:

  • Supabase hosts our database, real-time sync, authentication data and file storage.
  • OpenStreetMap-based services (OSRM for routing, Nominatim for place search) receive the coordinates you ask them to route or search.
  • Map tile providers receive the map area your device requests, along with your IP address, as with any map.
  • Google Fonts delivers typefaces used in the interface.
  • An SMS/email delivery provider used by our server to send notifications (section 9).

These providers act on our behalf and are not permitted to use your data for their own marketing.

9. SMS and email notifications

You may receive notifications such as an account-approval message at the phone number or email you gave us, and administrators may be notified of new registrations. These messages are sent from our server through a delivery service. The App on your phone does not send SMS and does not read your messages.

10. Storage on your device

The App stores small amounts of data on your device (local storage and cache) to keep you signed in, remember your theme and display settings, and keep map data available when your connection is poor. You can clear this by signing out or clearing the App's data in Android Settings. We do not use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking.

11. Retention and deletion

  • Account details are kept while your account is active, then deleted within a reasonable period after closure unless the law requires us to keep them.
  • Identity documents are kept only as long as needed for verification and audit, and can be deleted earlier on request.
  • Survey points form a shared dataset. If your account is deleted, your points may remain (without your personal profile) unless you ask for them to be removed.
  • Shared location history is kept only as long as needed for coordination and can be removed on request.
How to delete your account and data: email geo.roots.amrita.credits@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my GeoRoots account", from the email address or phone number linked to your account. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days, and tell you if anything must be kept by law. Your logged survey points are handled as described above.

12. Security

  • Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • Access to stored data is restricted by account type, and administrator access is limited to approved people.
  • Exports are locked by default and require administrator approval.
  • Registration is approval-based to limit misuse.

No online system is completely secure. Please keep your password private and tell us straight away if you think your account has been misused. If a personal-data breach occurs that affects you, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

13. Where data is stored

Data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, on servers that may be located outside India. By using the App you understand that your information may be processed in other countries, where we rely on the provider's security commitments and apply this policy to it.

14. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable law, you may:

  • ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you
  • ask us to correct or complete inaccurate data
  • ask us to delete your data, or withdraw your consent at any time
  • turn location sharing off in the App, and revoke device permissions in Settings
  • nominate someone to exercise your rights on your behalf if you are unable to
  • make a grievance to us, and if unresolved, to the Data Protection Board of India

Send requests to the contact in section 18. We aim to reply within 30 days. We may need to confirm your identity first.

15. Consent

We process your information based on your consent, which you give when you create an account, and when you turn on optional features such as location sharing. Amrita students and faculty additionally sign a consent form during registration. You can withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing it does not affect earlier processing, but we may then be unable to provide your account.

16. Children

GeoRoots is intended for adults aged 18 and over, and for students and faculty of higher-education institutions. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18. If you believe a child has registered, tell us and we will delete the account.

17. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App or the law changes. The "last updated" date at the top shows the current version. For important changes we will tell you in the App before they apply, and where the law requires it, we will ask for your consent again.
